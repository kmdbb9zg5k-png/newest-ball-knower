Ball Knower: Fantasy Football, NFL Picks & Football Trivia

Ball Knower is a football app for fans who want fantasy football, NFL game picks, football trivia, rankings, player research, NFL news, and solo football modes in one place. The official website is ballknowerofficial.com.

Users can create and join fantasy leagues, draft players, manage weekly lineups, review projections, make roster moves, follow matchups, and use research tools throughout the season. Ball Knower also includes submitted NFL picks with a verified pick record, football-knowledge challenges, daily trivia, and profile achievements that track activity over time.

Beyond traditional fantasy football, Ball Knower includes solo and franchise-style football experiences designed around team building and football decision-making. The product is built as an independent football platform and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the NFL or any NFL team.
