Ball Knower Privacy Policy

Effective September 3, 2026

Ball Knower is an independent football entertainment and fantasy platform. This policy describes the information used to operate Ball Knower and the choices available to users.

Information we collect

We may process account identifiers, profile details you choose to provide, profile photos, league activity, gameplay progress, preferences, basic product analytics, approximate country/region/city, and technical error information. Location analytics use a rotating anonymous identifier; Ball Knower does not intentionally store raw IP addresses as profile data.

Why we use it

We use this information to provide authentication, leagues, fantasy scoring, saved progress, profile features, security, support, reliability monitoring, and product improvement.

Service providers

Information may be processed by service providers required to operate Ball Knower, including hosting, database, authentication, analytics, sports-data, and email providers. Ball Knower does not sell personal information.

Your choices

You may request access or correction by contacting BallKnowerOfficial@gmail.com. Signed-in users can permanently delete their account from Ball Knower's Privacy screen. Account deletion removes the authentication identity, profile photo, and user-linked Ball Knower data. If the deleting user commissions a league containing other human members, commissioner control is transferred so those users are not stranded.

Delete your account in the app

Open Ball Knower → Privacy → Delete my account. The app requires a second confirmation before deletion. If you cannot access the app, email support from the address associated with your account.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to BallKnowerOfficial@gmail.com.
