Ball Knower NFL Picks

Make the call. Track the record.

Ball Knower lets users choose NFL game outcomes, review their choices, submit picks before kickoff, and build a verified prediction history over time.

How Ball Knower picks work

  • Select your NFL picks for available games.
  • Review your choices before submitting them.
  • Press Submit Picks to lock in the choices you want recorded.
  • Submit or update picks before the game locks at kickoff.
  • Track results as correct, missed, push or pending.
  • See lifetime correct-pick totals and full pick history in your Ball Knower Trophy Case.

Why the prediction record matters

Your submitted picks create a football-knowledge record you can review over time. Graded results feed the verified prediction history shown in your profile instead of relying on an editable local score.

Ball Knower picks are a football-knowledge feature, not a wagering product.
