Official feature guide

Ball Knower features

Ball Knower is a football app built around fantasy competition, football knowledge and proving your calls over time. The official site is ballknowerofficial.com.

Fantasy football

Create and join fantasy football leagues, draft rosters, manage weekly lineups, browse players, use fantasy research, handle transactions and follow weekly matchups. Ball Knower is designed to combine league play with deeper football intelligence instead of treating fantasy as a separate side feature.

NFL picks and prediction record

Make NFL game picks before kickoff, submit them, and build a verified prediction record. Correct graded picks contribute to your Ball Knower profile and Trophy Case, while your pick history lets you review what you chose and how each game finished.

Football trivia and The Gauntlet

Test football knowledge through trivia and challenge modes built around NFL facts, situations and decision-making. Difficulty levels and multiple challenge formats are designed to measure more than simple memorization.

Rankings, projections and player research

Use rankings, projections, player information, schedules, game logs and fantasy research tools to support draft, lineup, waiver and trade decisions.

Solo football modes

Play football-focused solo experiences including season and franchise-style modes, fantasy draft challenges, player-career experiences, Agent and Owner paths, and other ways to test roster building and football decision-making outside an online league.

NFL news and football information

Follow league-wide NFL news and football information inside the same product you use for fantasy, picks and trivia so research and gameplay stay connected.

Profile, ratings and Trophy Case

Your Ball Knower profile brings together verified progression, ratings and achievements. Prediction results, competitive accomplishments and other verified events can contribute to the record you build over time.

Ball Knower is an independent product and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the NFL or any NFL team.
