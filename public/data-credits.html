Independent estimates

Ball Knower ratings, simulation attributes, gameplay salary estimates and preseason projections are our estimates, not official league ratings, measured athletic skills, contract reports or guaranteed results. Sparse-data and offensive-line ratings rely primarily on role/experience assumptions. Published estimates are dated snapshots, not live injury forecasts. Draft rankings are model priorities, not observed average draft positions.

Live data and news

Live scoring and linked headlines are supplied through Tank01. Linked publishers retain their articles; Ball Knower does not republish their full articles or news images.
