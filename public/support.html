Ball Knower Support

For account, fantasy, gameplay, billing, privacy, or technical support, contact BallKnowerOfficial@gmail.com.

Reporting a bug

Include the screen you were on, what you tapped, what happened, your device/iOS version, and a screenshot when possible.

Account deletion

Signed-in users can permanently delete their account inside Ball Knower from Privacy → Delete my account. If you cannot access the app, contact support from the email address associated with your account.

Sports data

NFL data, projections, injury information, and fantasy scoring may be corrected or delayed. For time-sensitive decisions, confirm important information independently.
