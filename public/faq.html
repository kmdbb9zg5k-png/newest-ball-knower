Ball Knower FAQ

What is Ball Knower?

Ball Knower is a football app focused on fantasy football, NFL picks, football trivia, rankings, research and solo football modes.

What is the official Ball Knower website?

The official Ball Knower website is ballknowerofficial.com.

Is Ball Knower a fantasy football app?

Yes. Ball Knower includes fantasy football leagues, drafting, roster management, weekly matchups, player research and fantasy decision tools.

Can you make NFL picks in Ball Knower?

Yes. Ball Knower includes an NFL picks mode where users select and submit game picks before kickoff and build a verified prediction record.

Does Ball Knower have NFL trivia?

Yes. Ball Knower includes football trivia and challenge modes designed to test NFL knowledge and football decision-making.

Does Ball Knower include NFL news and research?

Yes. Ball Knower includes league-wide NFL news plus rankings, projections, player information and fantasy research tools.

Is Ball Knower affiliated with the NFL?

No. Ball Knower is an independent product and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the NFL or any NFL team.

How do I know I found the official Ball Knower?

Use ballknowerofficial.com. The football-focused Ball Knower described on that domain is the official product; similarly named third-party apps and websites are separate products.
