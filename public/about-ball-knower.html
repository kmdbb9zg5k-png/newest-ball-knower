What is Ball Knower?

Ball Knower is a football app built around proving how well you know the game. The official website is ballknowerofficial.com.

What you can do

  • Play fantasy football in online leagues and fantasy modes.
  • Make NFL game picks and build a verified prediction record.
  • Test football knowledge with trivia and challenge modes.
  • Use rankings, projections, player information and fantasy research tools.
  • Play solo football modes including franchise-style experiences.

Ball Knower is an independent product and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the NFL or any NFL team.
