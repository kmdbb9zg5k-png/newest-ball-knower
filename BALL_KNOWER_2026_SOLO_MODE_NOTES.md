# Solo Mode — Road to the Super Bowl

Implemented:
- Separate top-level Solo Mode tab.
- Independent 20-player roster builder under the $301.2M cap.
- K and P required.
- Smart auto-draft.
- Live team OVR, offense, defense, cap, draft grade and Ball Knower score.
- 17-game regular season against AI-built rosters.
- Playoff qualification gate.
- Wild Card, Divisional, Conference Championship and Super Bowl elimination path.
- Game-by-game scores and matchup explanations.
- End-of-run summary.
- New Run reset.
- Fixed evaluation engine mismatch so legal 4-OL / 3-DL-EDGE rosters are scored as complete.

Note: online league invites still require a shared backend and are not solved by this Solo Mode build.
